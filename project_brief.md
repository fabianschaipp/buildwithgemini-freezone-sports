# My agent: FreeZone Sports
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps athletes and outdoor enthusiasts make the most of their free time across Alpine hiking, cycling, and motorbike tours, while tracking Handball and NFL match days with rich route and game cards.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers favorite NFL/Handball teams, bike/motorbike profiles, fitness level, and past completed Alpine trails.
- Tools: Look up Alpine hiking/cycling/motorbike passes and trail conditions; look up Handball and NFL schedules/scores; generate gear checklists based on activity.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of Alpine mountain routes (passes, elevation, difficulty) and matchday fixtures rendered as rich A2UI cards and tables.
- Image gen: Generates scenic route previews, trail condition illustrations, and custom matchday spirit banners.
- Sandbox: Calculates elevation ascent rates (VAM), motorbike fuel ranges over mountain ascents, or training zones.

Units:
- European metric units by default (Celsius °C, kilometers km, meters m, km/h).

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for mountain route / wattage calculations, Google Maps integration for pass coordinates, Cloud Trace for latency tracking.
